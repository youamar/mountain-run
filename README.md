# Mountain Run: app prototype

Mountain Run is free Saturday trail runs in Selangor that care for the forest, in support of UN SDG 15.2. This is a clickable prototype of the runner's app, made for the APU Semester 5 project management module.

**Live prototype:** https://youamar.github.io/mountain-run/

## What you can try

- Pick a home trail and join a Saturday by making the three trail promises.
- Scan out at the finish to get counted in the safety headcount, then see your result as a race bib.
- Report a trail problem, check the live map, and learn the Leave No Trace rules.
- In **Profile**, the prototype switches let you see the error states, the interview research notes, and the trail status (open, muddy or resting). You can also switch the language to BM or 中文.

The app opens inside a phone frame on a laptop or projector and goes full screen on a phone. Everything is in one self-contained `index.html` file, with no build step and no server.

The elevation profiles are illustrative. They match each trail's distance and total climb, but they aren't GPS data.
